# ARTICLES_SBS

A reading list on stimulated Brillouin scattering (SBS) in gases, coherent and
stimulated Rayleigh–Brillouin spectroscopy, the kinetic theory of light
scattering, and the gas property data these models need.

Every entry links to the publisher (DOI) or to arXiv. Five articles are
published under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 license (CC BY 4.0) and
are included as PDFs in [`pdfs/`](pdfs/). They are marked **[PDF]** below. All
other items are copyrighted by their publishers and are listed by citation only.
Use the link or your library to get them.
